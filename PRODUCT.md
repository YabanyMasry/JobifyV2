# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Job seekers who want to automatically tailor their CV and cover letter to specific job descriptions.

## Product Purpose

Instantly generates tailored CVs, ATS match reports, and cover letters entirely client-side to protect privacy.

## Positioning

Client-side AI generation allows users to tailor their applications to specific job descriptions without sending their personal data to unknown servers.

## Operating Context

Job seekers paste a job description and their current CV details, generating new documents to copy/paste or save for applying to jobs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep existing React + Vite + Tailwind stack.
- Preserve the resume parsing logic and generated markdown results.

## Brand Commitments



## Evidence on Hand

Uses React, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, pdfjs-dist for CV import, react-markdown for result rendering.

## Product Principles

1. **Privacy First:** All data processing stays on the client side.
2. **Speed & Efficiency:** Instant generation saves the user time.
3. **High Fidelity:** The output must accurately reflect the job description and the user's authentic skills.
