# Brutalism Editorial Design System

## Core Aesthetic
- **Fonts**: Space Grotesk (headers, emphasis) and Space Mono (data, inputs, structural text).
- **Colors**: Monochrome (`#000000`, `#ffffff`) with a single hazard yellow accent (`#eab308`). No subtle grays except for disabled states.
- **Borders**: Thick, unapologetic borders (2px or 4px) around all interactive elements, containers, and sections.
- **Shadows**: Hard, solid black drop shadows (`4px 4px 0px 0px rgba(0,0,0,1)`). No blur.
- **Interaction**: Elements are tactile. Buttons press down to eliminate the shadow, simulating a physical machine switch.

## Voice & Tone
- Machine-like, terse, and functional.
- Uppercase headers and microcopy.
- Use brackets `[X]`, structural symbols `::`, and underscores `_` for labels.
- Examples: `EXECUTE_GENERATION`, `SYSTEM_PROFILE`, `SYS_DIAGNOSTIC`.

## Markdown Results Rendering
- Stark typography mimicking early printed manuals.
- `h1` with an underline and uppercase rendering.
- `h2`, `h3` with strong bold emphasis.
- Blockquotes formatted as highlighted system notices.
- Lists using square bullets, emphasizing brutalist geometry.
