# Jobify Main Surface

## Scope and Visitor Mode
**Scope:** Main application interface (Profile Editor, Generator, Results View).
**Mode:** Operate (Task-focused tool with reading components).

## Audience, Job, and Context
**Audience:** Job seekers.
**Job:** Tailoring their CV and cover letter to specific job descriptions instantly.
**Context:** They are applying for a job and need immediate, private, high-quality tailored documents.

## Direction Contract

**THESIS:** A highly-structured, stark machine for producing documents. No soft modern UI padding; the tool exposes its raw function like a printing press or command console. 

**OWN-WORLD:** Brutalism Editorial. Pure monochrome palette (white background, pitch-black borders and text) with one hazard-yellow accent for primary action. Typography contrasts a massive, rigid grotesque display face with a strict tabular monospace font for inputs and data. Solid borders, visible structural grid lines, sharp corners only.

**STORY:** The visitor understands they are operating a precise, mechanical text-processing tool. They load their raw data, feed in the target spec, and the machine prints an exact, tailored document. 

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The page is split by thick black structural lines. A stark monospace header spans the top. Below, a massive grotesque headline states the tool's purpose. The Profile, Generate, and Results sections are divided into rigid, bordered panels. Inputs are raw, unstyled rectangles with monospace labels. The primary Generate button is a large hazard-yellow block with black text.

**FORM:** Brutalism Editorial (User-pinned). Seed key: dbcfce17.
